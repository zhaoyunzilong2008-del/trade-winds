Trade Winds

Two islands. Two specialties. One fast boat. Coconut Cove is great at producing coconuts. Textile Bay is great at making cloth. Your job is to move each island's specialty to the other side before the clock runs out.

🥥 Coconut Cove
Dock here to load coconuts. Bring cloth back for big points.
🧵 Textile Bay
Dock here to load cloth. Bring coconuts here for big points.
🌩 Storms
Getting hit costs time and breaks your trade streak.
✨ Trade Crates
Grab floating bonuses while crossing the sea.

Move with WASD or arrow keys. Press Space near an island to trade. On phone, use the arrows and TRADE button.

What the game shows: each island gets more by focusing on the good it can produce at a lower opportunity cost and then trading for the other good. The gains come from specialization and exchange, not from every island trying to make everything itself.

That is the basic idea of comparative advantage. Even if one place is better at many things, trade can still help when relative opportunity costs differ.
